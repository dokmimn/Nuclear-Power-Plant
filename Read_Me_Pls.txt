Hi! It’s a bit of a long story. If you’re wondering why it’s called "AcidFactory" in the mod files, here’s the answer: I spent about two weeks developing a massive mod in Java, adding a bunch of new mechanics—turrets, acid, and much more. I struggled for a long time with the reactor, but once I moved on to other features, everything started to fall apart. Java just wouldn't let me work on the mod properly, and it was too late to switch to JavaScript (I did try). Eventually, tired of spending day and night battling endless bugs, I decided to put an end to it once and for all. I stripped out all the mechanics—the acid, turrets, pipes, factories, and new ores—leaving only this one component behind. So, the mod has lost its original core purpose, but I hope you still enjoy it! If you don't mind, please consider giving it a star on GitHub—I’d really appreciate it! Thanks for reading.
